## What we are asking

We would like to take photos or short videos of you while you carry out electrical tasks
(for example, wiring a switch board or testing a circuit). We will use them to:

- [ ] test whether our software can detect safety gear and tools in the picture,
- [ ] ask a small group of assessors to score the photos, to check whether our scoring guide helps
      them agree with each other,
- [ ] show examples in our project report and presentation (only if you tick this box).

## How we protect you

1. **Faces are blurred automatically** before any photo is stored. We do not store your name with
   the photo; we use a code such as `W07`.
2. Photos are stored on the project laptop and in a private project folder only. They are **never**
   put in a public code repository.
3. Only the project team, our faculty supervisor and the volunteer assessors will see the photos.
4. The software only **suggests** scores. It does **not** give you a certificate and it does not
   decide anything about you.
5. Taking part is voluntary. You can say no, or stop at any time, without giving a reason. If you
   withdraw, we will delete your photos within 7 days of your request.
