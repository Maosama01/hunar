## What we are asking

We would like to take photos or short videos of you while you carry out electrical tasks
(for example, wiring a switch board or testing a circuit). We will use them to:

- [ ] test whether our software can detect safety gear and tools in the picture,
- [ ] ask a small group of assessors to score the photos, to check whether our scoring guide helps
      them agree with each other,
- [ ] show examples in our project report and presentation (only if you tick this box).

## How we protect you

1. **Faces are blurred automatically** before any photo is stored. We do not store your name with
   the photo; we use a code such as `W07`.
2. Photos are stored on the project laptop and in a private project folder only. They are **never**
   put in a public code repository.
3. Only the project team, our faculty supervisor and the volunteer assessors will see the photos.
4. The software only **suggests** scores. It does **not** give you a certificate and it does not
   decide anything about you.
5. Taking part is voluntary. You can say no, or stop at any time, without giving a reason. If you
   withdraw, we will delete your photos within 7 days of your request.
6. We will delete all photos when the project ends, on or before: ____ / ____ / ______.

## Extra part for the video pilot (ceiling fan clips): voice and video

Fill this part only if the person records guided clips for the ceiling-fan pilot. A guided clip is a
short video (under one minute) of the person's hands and a fan part while they explain the part aloud.
Each purpose is separate: tick only the ones the person agrees to.

- [ ] **Record clips in the Hunar app.** We point the camera at your hands and the fan and try to keep
      your face out of the picture. Before the clip is stored, the app blurs any face it finds and
      **removes the sound**. The stored clip has no name, only a code such as `W07`.
- [ ] **Turn your speech into text on the project laptop.** The app writes down what you said and keeps
      that text with the clip, to show the assessor where you explained each part. It keeps everything
      you say, so please **do not say your name** or anyone else's in a clip.
- [ ] **Keep the sound of your recordings (no picture) for testing**, on the project laptop only, until
      the date in point 6 below. A team member listens to it and writes down exactly what you said, so
      we can measure how well the speech-to-text works. The original video files are deleted within 7
      days, once the sound has been copied out.
- [ ] **Send that sound to Bhashini**, the Government of India's language service, to compare its
      speech-to-text with ours. Only if you tick this box. Bhashini's own terms then apply to that copy.
- [ ] **Label still pictures from the stored clip** (faces blurred): team members mark whether the fan
      part and a hand are visible, to measure the picture check.
- [ ] **Show blurred, silent clips** in our report and presentation.

Your voice is personal data, like your face. Points 2 to 6 above apply to the clips and the sound too:
you can stop at any time, and we delete your clips, text and sound within 7 days if you ask.

## Extra part for the quiz pilot

Fill this part only if the person takes the trade quiz in a pilot session run by the team
(docs/remaining_tasks_guide.md, part B). The app also asks for its own consent on screen.

- [ ] **Take the quiz once in the Hunar app**, on your own phone or on one we lend you, connected to the
      project laptop. You log in with your mobile number and a PIN you choose. The quiz is a self-check:
      it is **not an exam, not a certificate**, and customers never see it. You may press "Don't know" or
      stop at any time.
- [ ] **Use your answers to improve the quiz.** Your answers and how long each one took, **without your
      name or mobile number**, are used to work out how hard each question is, and only totals (for
      example "120 electricians took part") appear in our project report.

Your mobile number and name stay only in the app's database on the project laptop. They are never put in
the code repository, shared, or used to contact you about anything else. You can erase your account at any
time in the app (Privacy, then "Erase my account now"), or ask us to. We delete the pilot's accounts by
the date in point 6 above.

## Extra part for work-story interviews

Fill this part only if a team member talks with the person about their work, to write a short "work
story" (a description of the work they do) for testing the software.

- [ ] **Talk with a team member about your work.** Nothing is recorded: the team member takes notes and
      later writes a few sentences in their own words. Your name, phone number, employer and exact place
      are never written down.
- [ ] **Use the written work story** to test whether our software suggests the right qualification, and
      quote it in our report.
